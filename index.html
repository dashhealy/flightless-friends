Flightless Friends

Penguins are one of the most fascinating creatures on Earth.

Beloved by all, they have much more charm than their airborne counterparts.

Lets learn all about penguins!

Penguins live in all sorts of places! Mainly in the Southern Hemisphere.

Only one species of penguin doesn't, the Galapagos Penguin lives just above the equator!

Little Penguins (or Fairy Penguins) live on the rocky coats of Australia and New Zealand.

Penguins also spend a lot of time in the Ocean!
