Flightless Friends

Penguins are one of the most fascinating creatures on Earth.

Beloved by all, they have much more charm than their airborne counterparts.

Lets learn all about penguins!

Penguins live in all sorts of places! Mainly in the Southern Hemisphere.

Only one species of penguin doesn't, the Galapagos Penguin lives just above the equator!

Little Penguins (or Fairy Penguins) live on the rocky costs of Australia and New Zealand.

Penguins also spend a lot of time in the Ocean!

Penguins get a lot of their food from the ocean.
They eat all sorts of things!

Like Krill,

Fish,

and Squid!

Penguins are incredibly important for managing the marine ecosystem.

They keep these species in balance, and prevent them from overpopulating!

Allowing the ecosystem to thrive.

But there's a problem...

Our planet is starting to heat up!

This is a bad thing for our little friends...

Big companies are polluting the air with STINKY smog

This is causing the ice caps to melt
Reducing the penguins' habitat and breeding season,

meaning less penguins :(

And hurting the rest of the ecosystem...

But we can help!

We can advocate for stronger environmental protections!

We can do lots at home to help too!

We can reduce our carbon footprint,

Reduce the amount of water we use

Recyle as much as we can,

And make more sustainable choices!

Here are some places where you can learn more about our penguin friends and get involed in conservation efforts!

WWF Penguin Page National Geographic Penguin Page Penguins International

And soon, we can make a much happier world for our flightless friends!
